Cinema, not protocol

[Placeholder — replace with your story] I started filming stories of people close to me and found in weddings the place where everything I love about cinema really happens: unscripted emotion, light that changes by the minute and people who aren't acting.

Today I film weddings all over Uruguay — Punta del Este, José Ignacio, Montevideo, Colonia, Carmelo and Rocha — and I travel for destination weddings abroad. I work in Spanish and English.

My approach is simple: arrive early, get to know your family, blend in with your guests and film without interrupting. No forced poses, no camera in your face. Then, in the edit, I craft a film with rhythm, music and color that will feel the same twenty years from now.
