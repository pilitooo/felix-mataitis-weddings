Destination wedding videographer in Uruguay

For couples who live abroad and chose Uruguay to say “I do”.

Why couples choose Uruguay

More and more couples from the United States, Europe, Brazil and Argentina are choosing Uruguay for their destination wedding: Atlantic beaches, boutique wineries, historic estancias, outstanding food and the ease of a small, safe and welcoming country.

As an English-speaking destination wedding videographer based in Uruguay, I'm with you from the first email: helping you choose the region, recommending trusted local vendors and shaping the coverage around a wedding weekend that often includes a welcome dinner, the wedding day and a farewell brunch.

The result is a film that tells not only your wedding, but the trip: the landscapes, the light of the Uruguayan coast and the people who travelled across the world to be with you.

Favourite destinations

  • Punta del Este & José Ignacio
  • Colonia del Sacramento & Carmelo
  • Wineries of Canelones & Pueblo Garzón
  • La Pedrera, Cabo Polonio & the Rocha coast
